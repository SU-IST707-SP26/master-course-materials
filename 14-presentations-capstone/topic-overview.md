## Learning Goals

This final week focuses on culminating student learning through professional project presentations, peer evaluation, and reflection on the complete machine learning journey.

1. **Deliver Professional ML Presentations**
   - Present complex technical projects to diverse audiences
   - Communicate business value and technical insights effectively
   - Handle questions and feedback professionally

2. **Evaluate Peer Work Constructively**
   - Assess technical quality and presentation effectiveness
   - Provide actionable feedback for improvement
   - Recognize diverse approaches to ML problem-solving

3. **Reflect on Learning Journey**
   - Synthesize knowledge gained throughout the course
   - Identify areas for continued learning and growth
   - Connect academic concepts to professional applications

## Assessment Components

### Final Presentation (Major Grade Component)
- **Technical Quality** (40%): Methodology, results, validation
- **Communication** (30%): Clarity, engagement, time management  
- **Innovation/Impact** (20%): Problem significance and creativity
- **Professionalism** (10%): Documentation, collaboration, reflection

### Peer Evaluation Participation
- **Quality of Feedback**: Constructive and detailed evaluations
- **Professional Engagement**: Respectful interaction and questions
- **Learning Contribution**: Sharing insights and perspectives
