## Showcase Event Structure

### Session Format

#### Individual Presentations
**Duration**: 15 minutes per student
- **Presentation**: 10-12 minutes
- **Q&A**: 3-5 minutes
- **Transition**: 2-3 minutes

#### Group Presentations  
**Duration**: 20 minutes per group
- **Presentation**: 15 minutes
- **Q&A**: 3-5 minutes
- **Transition**: 2 minutes

## Showcase Activities

### Pre-Presentation Setup
- [ ] **Technical Check**: 5 minutes before each session
- [ ] **Slide Loading**: All presentations loaded on presentation computer
- [ ] **Backup Plans**: USB drives and alternative presentation methods
- [ ] **Evaluation Forms**: Distributed to all attendees

### During Presentations
- [ ] **Timekeeper**: Designated person tracks presentation timing
- [ ] **Q&A Moderator**: Facilitates questions and discussion
- [ ] **Technical Support**: Available for any technical issues
- [ ] **Photography**: Document presentations (with permission)

### Post-Presentation Activities
- [ ] **Peer Evaluation**: Complete evaluation forms
- [ ] **Networking Time**: Informal discussion between presentations
- [ ] **GitHub Showcase**: Links to all project repositories
- [ ] **Award Voting**: Optional recognition categories
